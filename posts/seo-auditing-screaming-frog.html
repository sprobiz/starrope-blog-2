Technical SEO Auditing for Web Developers with Screaming Frog

Primary Value Automate site-wide scanning to identify search indexation issues

Publishing high-quality content is only half the battle. If your site has technical indexation errors, search engines like Google will penalize your rankings. **Screaming Frog SEO Spider** is a desktop crawler that scans your site just like a search bot, letting you find and fix technical issues before they hurt your traffic.

1. Auditing Redirects and Broken Links

Start a crawl by entering your domain name. Look at the **Response Codes** report to find broken links (404 errors) or server issues (500 errors). Update these links to point to active URLs to keep users and crawlers moving smoothly.

"Keep your site's crawl depth shallow. Critical pages should always be accessible within 3 clicks of the home page."

2. Analyzing Meta Tag Integrity

Check the Page Titles and Meta Description tabs to find pages with missing, duplicate, or overly long tags. Keeping titles under 60 characters and descriptions under 160 characters prevents text truncation in search results.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Screaming Frog free to use?

The free version crawls up to 500 URLs per site, which is ample for small blogs and landing pages.

Can Screaming Frog crawl JavaScript sites?

Yes, the paid version supports rendering JavaScript using a headless Chrome browser to index SPA applications.

How do I find redirect loops?

Navigate to the 'Redirection' report tab after a crawl completes to identify chains and infinite loops.
