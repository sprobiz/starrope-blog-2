How to Cancel Claude Pro Subscription (Refund & Auto-Billing Off)

Author: Starrope • June 5, 2026 • Read Time: ~3 min
Header Premium Banner

Are you looking to stop your monthly $20 Claude Pro membership to avoid automatic credit card charges? Follow this quick, step-by-step guide to cancel your subscription immediately and understand the official refund rules.

Web Interface Profile Initials → Billing → Cancel Subscription → Confirm Pro features remain active until the end of your billing cycle. Partial refunds are not supported.

1. Cancel Claude Pro via Desktop Web Browser

If you subscribed to Claude Pro directly using your credit card on the Anthropic website, use this method:

  1. Sign in to your Claude.ai account.
  2. Click your profile icon (initials) in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar.
  3. Select 'Billing' from the popup menu.
  4. Click the 'Cancel Subscription' link. The system will guide you through confirmation screens to complete the cancellation.
"Once canceled successfully, your billing status will display 'Pending Cancellation' which confirms you will not be charged again next month."

2. Disable Billing on Mobile Devices (App Store & Google Play)

Users who purchased their subscriptions via mobile apps must terminate renewal settings directly through their OS settings:

  • Apple App Store (iOS): Open Settings → Tap your Name/Apple ID → Subscriptions → Select Claude → Tap Cancel Subscription.
  • Google Play Store (Android): Open Play Store → Profile Icon → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions → Claude Pro → Cancel Subscription.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Will I lose my Pro access immediately after cancellation?

No. You will retain full access to Claude Pro benefits, including higher message limits and Claude 3.5 Sonnet, until the end of your current 30-day billing cycle. No further payments will be processed.

Q2. Can I get a refund for unused days?

Anthropic does not offer prorated or partial refunds for billing periods that have already started. If you have unused usage and believe you qualify for exceptional refund cases under local customer laws, you can contact Anthropic support.

Q3. What happens to my chat history after downgrading?

All your conversations and project files remain saved in your account profile. You will not lose your history, though your message limit and speed will be scaled back to the free tier levels.
